## 🛡️ Our Mission

Vibranium Audits exists to empower organizations to securely and confidently adopt blockchain, smart contract, and dApp technology. Founded in 2021 by former cybersecurity faculty from the University of Greenwich, we combine academic rigor with battle-tested offensive security to ship audits teams actually trust.

## 🔧 What We Do

- **Smart Contract Audits** — Manual + automated review of Solidity, Rust, Move, Cairo, and Vyper contracts.
- **Blockchain & Protocol Audits** — End-to-end review of L1s, L2s, bridges, and consensus logic.
- **Penetration Testing** — Web, mobile, infrastructure, and dApp pentests against modern threat models.
- **KYC Verification** — Founder identity verification trusted by exchanges, launchpads, and investors.
- **Bug Bounty Programs** — Design, triage, and run continuous bounties with our researcher network.
- **Proof of Reserves** — Cryptographic attestations that on-chain assets match off-chain liabilities.
